    Covers the City of Houston only. Unincorporated Harris County (Channelview, Sheldon, Crosby, etc.) and other cities are not included in this feed.

    Get official alerts on your phone

    • Ready Harris Alerts: Harris County's official text/call/email alerts, including shelter-in-place orders. Sign up for your home address.
    • Wireless Emergency Alerts: make sure emergency/public-safety alerts are turned on in your phone's notification settings.
    • Phone push from this tool: install the ntfy app and subscribe to the topic configured in this project's monitor (see the README).

    Primary sources

    • AirNow (official air quality)
    • Harris County scanner feeds (Broadcastify)
